Gentle & Non-Invasive Assistive Technology

Real-Time Focus & Sensory De-Escalation Eyewear

Vori is an ultra-lightweight (<90g) smart eyewear designed for neurodivergent children (ADHD & ASD). It detects early biometric signs of sensory overload and attention drift, presenting subtle, calming visual cues in peripheral vision to prevent meltdowns before they occur.
